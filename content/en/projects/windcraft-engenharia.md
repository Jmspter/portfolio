## Problem

Windcraft Engenharia needed an institutional presence that conveyed the company's credibility in engineering services, but the old site was static and hard to keep up to date.

## Solution

I developed the official website with Sinatra (Ruby), a lightweight framework well suited for this kind of project: institutional pages, a showcase of services and projects, a contact form and a simple content maintenance structure. The choice kept the deployment lean and performance high.

## What I learned

Sinatra reinforced that not every project needs a full framework. I organized the app with well-separated routes, views and business logic, and learned to integrate forms, email sending and assets in a simple, direct way.
